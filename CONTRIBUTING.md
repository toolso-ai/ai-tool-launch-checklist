# Contributing

Improvements should help someone make a specific launch decision or perform a verifiable action.

## Propose a change

- Describe the problem, the affected checklist ID, and a concrete improvement.
- Include a current primary source and check date for claims about platform requirements. Explain any uncertainty or access limits.
- Keep the six milestones and 30 stable IDs consistent across the English and Chinese files. Update the README copies when a checklist changes.
- Identify your relationship to a product or platform when relevant. Use safe, fictional examples or material you have permission to share.
- For translations, preserve action meaning and flag wording that needs native review.

## Scope

Useful contributions include clearer instructions, broken-link corrections, missing dependencies, accessibility improvements, and better examples. Avoid promotional directory lists, unrelated product links, reciprocal links, vote requests, fabricated results, credentials, or private customer data.

Check the repository's current discussion and issue state before submitting. Do not repost a removed contribution or send repeated requests. Maintainers review contributions when available; no response time is promised.

## Before submitting

- Open the changed Markdown files and follow their local links.
- Confirm all 30 template items remain unchecked and IDs are unique.
- Confirm English and Chinese checklist IDs match.
- Check that examples remain clearly fictional, with unmeasured metrics marked as such.
- Describe what you checked and what you could not verify.

Contributions are proposed under the repository's [MIT License](LICENSE). Submit only material you have the right to share under those terms.
