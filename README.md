# AI Tool Launch Checklist

A practical launch template for AI tool founders, developers, and small teams. Plan six milestones from 30 days before launch to 30 days after it, with 30 actions covering product claims, launch assets, channel rules, measurement, support, and review.

Maintained by Toolso.AI, an AI tools directory. The checklist is a planning resource; its examples describe fictional work and contain no measured launch results.

[简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)

## Start with your own launch

1. Copy [CHECKLIST.md](CHECKLIST.md) into your project notes or a private issue. No installation is required.
2. Fill in the launch header. Record actual work dates separately from planned milestone dates.
3. Add an owner, due date, dependency, status, evidence, and next action to each item. Use [the fictional launch record](examples/launch-record.example.md) as a formatting example.
4. Keep an item unchecked until its evidence exists. Use a hold state when approval or a platform requirement is missing. Adjust the dates when a real dependency needs longer.

Milestones do not override platform rules, review times, observation windows, or release approvals. Choose relevant channels and check their rules. This template does not guarantee rankings, backlinks, or revenue.

## Track links and results

Give each channel one primary destination. Keep UTM values consistent, using lowercase source names and a stable campaign and content identifier. [The example link register](examples/utm-links.example.csv) uses a reserved example domain and fictional values. Replace them before use. Do not add campaign parameters to canonical URLs or expose private information in query strings.

Record an actual URL and visible status after publishing. A saved draft, submitted listing, approved listing, visible link, accepted indexing request, and indexed page are different states. At review time, use matching periods and denominators, exclude test traffic, and distinguish missing data from an observed zero. Keep customer records and credentials outside public copies of this template.

## T-30: Define the launch
- [ ] T30-01: Document the audience, input, useful output, access conditions, and practical limits.
- [ ] T30-02: Define the primary outcome, measurement window, baseline, and test exclusions.
- [ ] T30-03: Shortlist channels and record each channel's audience, eligibility, rules, and review requirements.
- [ ] T30-04: Confirm existing accounts and owners; keep credentials in a password manager.
- [ ] T30-05: Assign delivery, approval, support, and incident owners; list launch-blocking dependencies.

## T-14: Prepare the launch kit
- [ ] T14-01: Check the landing page's workflow, limits, pricing basis, support route, and main link.
- [ ] T14-02: Check mobile layout, metadata, canonical, crawl access, sitemap coverage, and destination URLs.
- [ ] T14-03: Capture readable workflow screenshots and any required demo; confirm privacy and reuse rights.
- [ ] T14-04: Approve consistent product descriptions, categories, pricing sources, and relationship disclosures.
- [ ] T14-05: Register channel links with consistent UTM values; keep campaign parameters out of canonical URLs.

## T-7: Rehearse
- [ ] T07-01: Test the external-link-to-useful-output journey in a fresh session with safe sample data.
- [ ] T07-02: Verify attribution and the outcome event; remove duplicates and document test exclusions.
- [ ] T07-03: Recheck account access, product ownership, channel eligibility, links, disclosures, and approvals.
- [ ] T07-04: Rehearse support, escalation, cancellation information, and payment checks in a suitable test environment.
- [ ] T07-05: Record a go-or-hold decision for each channel and its unresolved dependencies.

## T0: Launch day
- [ ] T00-01: Recheck the promised workflow and material faults; obtain the required release and content approvals.
- [ ] T00-02: Publish permitted, approved content; record the actual URL, account, time, timezone, and visible status.
- [ ] T00-03: Check live copy, destination, disclosures, and UTM; separate submission, approval, link visibility, and indexing.
- [ ] T00-04: Where applicable, request search discovery or indexing and retain the response; verify indexing later.
- [ ] T00-05: Handle substantive questions and faults; avoid coordinated votes, repetitive promotion, and unsolicited message blasts.

## T+7: Review the first week
- [ ] T07P-01: Compare visits and useful actions over the same window; separate tests, missing data, and observed zeroes.
- [ ] T07P-02: Group feedback by understanding, activation, output quality, pricing, and trust; retain non-sensitive evidence.
- [ ] T07P-03: Assign a short correction list with owners, priorities, and verification criteria; recheck changed claims.
- [ ] T07P-04: Review pending submissions through each platform's process; avoid duplicates or reposting removed content.
- [ ] T07P-05: Choose what to continue, change, or hold; recheck community rules before further participation.

## T+30: Plan the next cycle
- [ ] T30P-01: Compare acquisition, continued use, support effort, and commercial outcomes with explicit periods and denominators.
- [ ] T30P-02: Separate invoices, collected payments, refunds, and unverified attribution when reconciling commercial figures.
- [ ] T30P-03: Allocate time and budget to the next experiments with reasons and stop conditions.
- [ ] T30P-04: Refresh documentation, directory records, support answers, and actual link or indexing status where authorized.
- [ ] T30P-05: Record decisions, owners, evidence, and changes; obtain permission before sharing customer material or public resources.
